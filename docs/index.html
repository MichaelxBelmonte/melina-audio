01 / THE SYSTEM

Noise is everywhere.
The voice is still there.

Melina runs a causal audio pipeline between your microphone and headphones. It detects speech, reduces competing noise, fits the result for clarity, and controls the output — continuously, without sending the conversation away.

01

Capture

Select any compatible microphone. Melina keeps a native 48 kHz mono stream.

02

Focus

Choose from up to nine local engines, from classic DSP to neural enhancement.

03

Deliver

Speech fitting, compression, and limiting prepare a controlled headphone feed.

03 / PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE

Your conversations stay yours.
