Quick protocol
  1. Listen to a difficult sentence in RAW, then every output without changing the volume.
  2. Score understood words, naturalness, and metallic discomfort from 1–5.
  3. Open benchmark.csv: a model above 75% has little headroom; above 100% is not sustainable in real time for that run.
  4. CPU figures do not measure perceived quality; the final choice must include the intended listener.
